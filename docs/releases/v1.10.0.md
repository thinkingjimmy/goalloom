# Goalloom 1.10.0

Private release candidate. The macOS arm64 package passed signing, notarization, Gatekeeper, ticket, package-budget and isolated native acceptance. Production cutover and Windows 11 acceptance are pending. This file does not announce availability or open purchases.

## Account services

- Plus adds encrypted sync and built-in AI through an external Stripe purchase managed with RevenueCat. Local work and your own AI key remain available without a subscription.
- The planned price is USD 8.99 per month or USD 79 per year, inclusive of applicable tax. There are no discounts or automatic overage charges.
- One explicit account trial lasts eight days. A confirmed purchase consumes trial eligibility. Cancellation keeps the current paid period; a billing-cycle change takes effect at the next renewal. A failed automatic renewal has at most 72 hours of grace from the original renewal boundary.
- Account settings show service status and data deadlines. When service ends, eligible retained data can be retrieved as an independent local copy within 30 days. Replacement of the current workspace requires an explicit choice and a protective backup. Online deletion remains due within seven days after the applicable deadline.
- Local backups remain independent of account services. Restricted disaster-recovery copies follow the infrastructure provider's retention policy. They are not a user recovery service. Infrastructure restoration must reapply deletion records before access resumes.

## Update

Workspace format is v14. Updates preserve the application identity, user-data location, tasks and settings. Keep a verified local backup before installing this private candidate. Installers and their signing status must be checked against the final acceptance record; no package is accepted by these notes.

The production billing schema blocks older clients from sync even when new admission is closed. Do not apply that cutover until a usable new client is ready and the maintenance plan is reviewed. Use the same account and sync password on each computer, and update participating computers together. This candidate does not include a mobile release.

- 账号明确领取后可试用八天。确认购买后，试用资格即被消耗。取消订阅保留当前已付周期；周期切换从下次续订生效。自动续费失败时，宽限最多为原续订截止后的 72 小时。

# Goalloom 1.8.0

## What's new

- **Choose how to start.** A new board asks whether to use it on this computer or sign in with Google. A second computer signs in, enters the sync password, and opens the board.
- **Sync across computers.** Sign in with Google and set a sync password. Your board stays in sync between your computers. Your data is encrypted before it leaves your device. The board still works if you skip sync.
- **Sync settings and the sign-in page.** Sync sits with your Google account and shows whether it is working. After you sign in, a page tells you whether it succeeded.
- **Notices you can close.** The time-zone notice and backup notices can be closed. Hints that only repeated a control are gone.
- **Shorter review dates.** Week and month reviews leave out the year, unless the dates cross into another year.
- **A second computer asks first.** If that computer already has items, you choose whether to use the other board or merge both. You can export a copy before you decide.

## Fixes

- **Turning on sync keeps your board.** You are not sent back to the beginning. Your board comes back on its own.
- **Old sync notices go away.** After the board is restored, leftover sync notices are cleared on their own.
- A task you add while that choice is still finishing is kept. This computer's board stays as it is until the step is done.
- A long note can sync. If one change cannot be sent, changes from the other computer still arrive.
- Tasks brought onto a different calendar stay on the period you can see.
- Signing in with a different Google account keeps the sync setup already saved on this computer. A sign-in that stops early returns to the sign-in button.
- If this computer cannot unlock its saved sync data, that data stays locked. The board still works.

## 更新说明

- **先选择怎么开始。** 新的看板会问你是在这台电脑上直接使用，还是用 Google 登录。另一台电脑登录后输入同步密码，就会直接进入看板。
- **在电脑之间同步。** 用 Google 登录，并设置一个同步密码。看板会在几台电脑之间保持同步。数据在离开这台设备之前就会加密。不开启同步时，看板照常使用。
- **同步设置和登录结果页。** 同步和 Google 账号在一起，并显示是否在同步。登录之后，页面会告诉你这次是否成功。
- **提示可以关掉。** 时区和备份的提示可以关闭。只重复控件说明的提示也少了。
- **复盘日期更短。** 周复盘和月复盘的日期不再显示年份，除非这段日期跨年。
- **第二台电脑会先问你。** 如果那台电脑上已经有条目，你可以选择使用另一块看板，或把两边合并。决定之前可以先导出一份副本。

## 修复

- **打开同步后，看板还在。** 不会被送回最初的设置。看板会自动恢复。
- **恢复之后，旧的同步提示会自己消失。**
- 这个选择还没完成时新加的任务会保留。这一步完成之前，这台电脑上的看板保持原样。
- 较长的说明可以同步。某一条改动发不出去时，另一台电脑的改动仍会到达。
- 带到另一套日历上的任务会留在你能看到的周期里。
- 用另一个 Google 账号登录，会保留这台电脑上已经保存的同步设置。登录中途停下时，会回到登录按钮。
- 如果这台电脑打不开已保存的同步数据，那些数据会保持锁定。看板仍可使用。

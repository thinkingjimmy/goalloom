# 二级 todo

> Independent tasks with persistent two-level ownership. Only tasks scheduled in the same horizon/period form a local outline. Goal links remain a separate graph.

## Product rules

- Drop a task on the middle of a root or any of its children to join that root's horizon/period at the end. One border encloses the complete displayed group. A middle drop within the same group leaves its order unchanged.
- Child row edges insert into that group at the shown position, including the first and last slots; the insertion line starts at the child checkbox, 24px beyond the root checkbox, including the column's flow gutter. Root row edges sort outside the whole group with a full-width line. Dropping a locally nested child at a root edge in its own placement makes it independent at that position, including before or after its own group. Detachment and insertion share one write and one undo. Group borders and insertion lines are mutually exclusive.
- Pointer drag cards stay 12px below the pointer to expose insertion feedback; keyboard drag cards keep their existing row anchor.
- Keep the 6px drag threshold and 24px indent. Each vertical sorting margin is the smaller of 16px and one quarter of the full row height, leaving at least the middle half for nesting, including 32px single-line rows. The nesting zone covers the left 60% of the row width (at least 200px). Clip these zones to the visible viewport without moving their boundaries within the row.
- Nesting a root with children flattens its complete live group under the target root: insert the source first, then its children in their current order, at the selected child slot or at the end. All members move to the target horizon/period and inherit its goals/colors. A nested task cannot receive nested children. Keyboard menus provide equivalent nest/unnest actions.
- In any current/future column or Later inline entry, Tab indents the unsaved draft by 24px under the last displayed task, or that task's root if it is already nested. Folded completed sections are excluded. Enter creates one child and keeps the same root for continuous entry; Shift+Tab restores the independent draft and its previous flow choice. No third level is created. With no eligible preceding root, or while already nested, Tab keeps native focus navigation. IME composition keys do not change levels.
- Nesting changes neither draft text nor caret selection. Closing/reopening and period switching retain the period's draft and root identity. Nested drafts display their container and inherited flow colors without an independent flow picker or AI matching; Later remains colorless. A moved/archived/deleted/nested/expired container rejects saving without discarding the draft. Delayed replies clear only the submitted revision and never overwrite subsequent text or outdenting.
- While editing an existing title on the current/future board or Later, Tab nests an independent task under the immediately preceding displayed task (or its local root). Shift+Tab detaches a child, including a separately scheduled child, in its own placement. Use the same eligible-root rules as inline creation; do not cross completed sections or create a third level. With no eligible target, or at the requested level, keep native Tab navigation. IME and modified Tab do not change levels. Each level change uses the existing atomic nest/unnest operation and undo, independently of the title edit; Escape cancels only the title draft. Keep the editor, text, selection and focus during writes/reordering, defer completed-section migration until editing ends, and retain the draft on failed/unknown writes. Suppress repeated level changes while a write is pending.
- Successful nesting stays quiet for drag, menu and recovered receipts. Root-edge detachment also stays quiet; explicit menu/keyboard unnest feedback is unchanged. Keep keyboard/command undo and its confirmation, plus existing failure feedback.
- The selector searches current, future and Later tasks, including periods outside the displayed board. Expired or changed targets are rejected.
- Nested tasks inherit all current goal parents and flow colors from their root. Their own outgoing goal links, text, dates and lifecycle remain independent.
- Owner decision, 2026-10-19: ownership names a specific parent task, independently of planning horizon/period. Moving or sorting a child between planning placements retains ownership. Explicit unnest/reparent actions change ownership; there is still only one task placement and at most two levels.
- Keep each placement-local outline in its sorted position, including completed members. Completed folding applies to local groups only. Hidden same-period roots retain a two-line lifecycle context; cross-period children use an unindented ordinary row with a clickable, localized parent link, without a duplicate parent card. Details omit the owning-task name and ownership controls; the board outline and cross-period links provide that context. Existing board menus and title-edit shortcuts still make a task independent. See [completed-task layout](completion-feedback.md#已完成任务显示).
- Root moves carry only live children in the root's source horizon/period. Independently scheduled children keep their placements and ownership. Rollover, backlog arrangement and review decisions act on source-period local groups; a remote child never moves its parent. A current-period hold blocks only the affected local group.
- Unnesting or deleting the root materializes its current inherited goal parents; a colored root with no goal parents leaves detached children colorless. Eligible item restore retains the child's independent placement; moved/reparented children are not reclaimed. Scoped creation restore retains its existing changed-container warning.
- A child moved alone across the Later boundary becomes independent; entering Later also clears its goal parents. A root entering Later carries its local outline; children elsewhere stay in place and become independent with their inherited goals. All effects share one undo. No ownership link crosses the Later boundary.
- Flow dots/colors still inherit from the parent. Draw goal → parent → child directly, suppressing inherited duplicate incoming lines; preserve the child's outgoing links. Same-period ownership uses the 24px gutter and the existing 5px rounded connector style. Starting vertically at the parent dot must not flatten the bend into the child dot. Clamp each corner to its own adjoining segments; preserve dot-edge endpoints and the original stroke/color/highlight rules. Cross-column lines retain normal clipping/preview/settings rules.
- Delete only the selected task. Undo restores owned fields and relations atomically without overwriting later text/status edits. Item restore never steals moved or reparented children.
- Later supports nesting but has no flow colors or new goal links. Past periods retain the outline and existing lifecycle/detail actions.

## Engineering contract

- Schema/dataset v10: optional legacy/default-null placement nesting parent, body-free hierarchy history and v3 owned nesting effects. v10 sits on the Phase 0 v9 chain and mirrors the column and triggers onto `base_*`. v8 and Phase 0 v9 upgrade in place after a protective backup; v1–v7 keep the verified protective-copy and explicit-confirmation workflow. `nest` / `unnest` map to SyncOp `item.nest` (placement dimension, plus relation and color actions in the same op).
- Nesting and explicit goal links are distinct. Shared effective-topology rules drive membership/AI context; direct explicit/nesting edges drive line drawing and chain traversal. Local grouping compares horizon and period, separately from ownership, across renderer, transactional ordering and visible/base replay. Validate combined cycles, two levels and the Later boundary in authoritative writes and import. Existing fields, dataset v11, SyncOp v1 and historical receipts remain unchanged. Canonical replay validates final parent availability/two-level shape/Later boundaries, rejects owner deletion with unseen live children and clears deleted ownership before rebuilding.
- Nest/unnest are finite generation/version-guarded commands. `nest.beforeId` optionally inserts before a live child of the destination root; absent/null appends. Validate its group, horizon and period in the write transaction. Membership, insertion, incoming-link and color changes share one receipt and one undo. Existing inherited links materialized on detach retain their source provenance; no dataset or sync format change is needed.
- `unnest.beforeId` optionally selects a root insertion slot in the child's existing placement; explicit null selects the tail. Omission retains the existing menu/keyboard placement behavior. Validate the insertion neighbor in the same transaction as detachment.
- Group nesting reparents children before nesting their former root so SQLite and sync actions remain valid. The receipt records final sibling positions; one undo restores the original outline atomically.
- Single-task `createPlan` can also carry the same guarded nesting contract. Global smart capture can select one same-period root; ready and late ownership remain part of the creation undo and scoped restore. Inline AI and multi-item plans keep their existing behavior.
- `create.nesting` optionally carries `parentId`, `expectedParentVersion` and `expectedParentPlacementVersion`. The write transaction requires an available, unarchived, uncancelled root in the exact requested horizon/current-or-future period; independent goal refs/color, AI capture and non-null root insertion are rejected. Creation skips root auto-placement and records existing `create` plus `nesting` effects in one operation. Sync emits `item.create` at its independent position followed by `item.nest` in the same SyncOp. Undo preserves later text; scoped restore derives group provenance from the original immutable creation receipt, restoring independently with a warning if the group changed. Dataset v11 and SyncOp v1 are unchanged.
- Root ordering precedes child ordering. Flatten groups into the existing virtual list; preserve task identity, drafts, focus and scroll. Draw group borders from virtual offsets, including an unmounted root, without mounting extra group members. Borders ignore pointer events and clip to the column viewport. Cancellation, focus loss, period changes and generation changes clear previews.
- Board title editing and inline creation share `precedingNestingRoot` for displayed-root selection. The title field submits the existing versioned commands, serializes pending level writes with Enter/blur commits, and keeps its content wrapper stable when a remote-parent link disappears. Column retains the edited local group's completed-section membership until editing ends. Virtual-row navigation leaves text-editor keys to the input.
- All user-visible copy is translated into Chinese, English, Japanese, Spanish and French.

## Failure cases recorded before implementation

- Details repeat the owning-task name or expose a redundant ownership/detach control after its removal. Verify absence in all five languages while preserving stored ownership, inherited goals/colors and the board's existing independent-task action.
- Same-placement root-edge drops reject a local child, retain its old ownership, insert at the wrong root slot or split another group. Cover today head/middle/tail, own-group edges, cancel/reject/unknown receipts, inherited goals, persistence and one-step undo without changing text/status. Child-edge sorting and cross-placement ownership must retain their existing behavior.
- Existing-title Tab: wrong preceding root, folded/history/cross-period target, a third level or a topology cycle; Shift+Tab changes a remote child's placement. Reordering, unnesting a completed child or removing its parent link must not remount the editor, discard unsaved text/selection, or commit it on a synthetic blur. Cover native Tab fallback, IME/modifiers, delayed/failed/unknown receipts, Enter/blur after a pending write, title-only Escape and one-step level undo preserving later text.
- Cross-period ownership: moving/sorting a child silently detaches it, moves its remote parent, or displays a duplicate parent card in the destination. Direct lines must follow goal → parent → child without inherited sibling lines.
- Moving a root carries remotely scheduled children; moving a child during rollover/backlog carries its parent from another horizon/period; an unrelated hold blocks a separately scheduled group.
- Later transitions leave cross-Later ownership, lose inherited goals on children left behind, or fail to restore placement/ownership atomically on undo. Same-period groups moved together into Later retain their local outline.
- Cross-period undo/restore steals a reparented child, relocates it to the parent, overwrites text/status, fails import validation, or diverges between visible/base encrypted replay. Sorting and insertion must use placement-local groups on every write/read path.
- Nesting B with children C/D under A leaves a third level, reverses sibling order, splits periods, loses archived/cancelled members, changes content/status or omits a member from the receipt.
- Group insertion ignores its selected sibling, splits the source group or partially reparents children when its insertion neighbor is invalid.
- Group nesting accepts itself, its own children or a combined goal/outline cycle, partially commits on failure, duplicates a retry or fails JSON/SQLite and encrypted sync round trips.
- One undo fails because effects record intermediate neighbors, restores children before B is independent, overwrites later edits, steals a subsequently moved child or flattens children acquired after a previous unnest.
- Inline Tab creation: Tab changes the caret/text, chooses an item from a folded section or another period, creates a third level, or follows a different parent after a reorder. Enter must keep the selected root and Shift+Tab must restore the independent draft's flow choice. Native Tab navigation remains available with no target or at level two; IME keys never change nesting.
- Inline creation partially saves before nesting fails, runs automatic root placement, accepts a moved/archived/deleted/nested parent, or lets AI add independent incoming goals. Cover all seven columns and future periods, stale versions, period expiry, retries/unknown receipts and typing or outdenting before a save reply.
- Undoing inline creation requires two steps, loses later text edits, or scoped restore loses the original group after an unrelated root is added. JSON/SQLite validation and encrypted replay must accept the single create-plus-nesting receipt, retain parent-relative sibling order and restore independently with a warning when the original group changes.
- Hovering a child rejects its root as a nesting target, or outlines only one row instead of the complete visible group. A scrolled, unmounted root must still have a clipped group preview without mounting its children outside the virtual window.
- Child-edge insertion appends instead of inserting, uses a full-width root marker, or saves membership and order as separate operations. Cover first/middle/last positions, cross-group/cross-column insertion, same-group reordering and a single undo.
- A child insertion line applies 24px from the row edge and omits the flow gutter, placing its start beside the root checkbox. Check its actual start against the child checkbox in current/future columns and Later, including an unmounted root; root insertion must retain its full width.
- A zero-length starting horizontal segment removes the child's lower rounded corner, a short segment overshoots its endpoint, or the curve leaves the 24px gutter. Check the rendered bend, endpoints, stroke and all four appearances; shared goal-link/drag routes must retain rounded corners.
- The pointer overlay covers the insertion line. Keep it below the pointer so the line stays visible; keyboard overlays retain their row anchor.
- A same-group middle drop changes order; a deleted/reparented insertion neighbor or stale parent partially changes membership, relations or colors. Failed, unknown and cancelled drops must clear feedback and keep the receipt rules.
- Middle-row drops reorder instead of nesting; edge drops nest accidentally; invalid/hidden/history targets write; cancellation or a stale generation leaves an outline preview.
- Fixed 16px edge zones consume a 32px row's nesting area. Native drops at 35% and 65% of the row height must preview nesting; committing across automatic parent groups must nest, retain focus and support undo.
- Edge insertion lines and persisted order must match in either direction, including adjacent unchanged slots, the last sibling and one-step undo; ending or cancelling clears all drag markers.
- Nesting changes text/status/due dates, loses outgoing links, keeps old incoming links/colors or allows a third level.
- Inherited parents/colors become stale; a goal edge and nesting edge form a cycle; inherited edges are editable on a child or counted as an additional task.
- Mixed-status groups split apart, archived/cancelled roots hide active children, or virtual rows lose focus and geometry.
- Group moves/rollover run twice, partially commit, change completion timestamps or ignore an unfinished member's hold.
- Unnest/delete loses inherited parents, children or their outgoing links; restore steals independently moved/reparented children.
- Undo overwrites unrelated edits, restores an occupied color, skips a dependency guard, partially reverses a group or loses expired-period holds. New standalone colors or goal parents after departure must produce a definitive conflict before re-nesting.
- JSON/SQLite restore loses nesting/provenance, accepts invalid parents/placements/cycles or forged effects; legacy upgrade mutates the original database before confirmation.
- Retry duplicates a write, unknown outcomes lose the receipt, language switching leaves untranslated copy or reduced motion still animates.
- A cleared title silently discards a root or child with outline dependencies; inherited information has no readable popover surface; future search is limited to the displayed period.
- An archived/completed container adds a required AI dependency beyond the bounded context limit; completed relationships inflate visible omission counts.

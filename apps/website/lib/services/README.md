# lib/services/

> Parent: [Website map](../../README.md).

- `release.ts`: Dated prices, finalized policy v1.0 values, public client facts and fail-closed publication gates. No duplicate provider protocol/cache snapshot, provider secrets or billing identities.
- `types.ts`: Localized plan cards, annual-savings templates, information/trial labels, shared common-answer facts, billing-group labels, stable FAQ identities and typed billing-policy sections without a standalone launch-banner field.
- `metadata.ts`: Pricing metadata and noindex billing metadata.
- `continuation.ts`: Finite BILL-12 fields, exact environment/transport pairing, fixed app/loopback targets and session-only recovery capped at 24 hours; no account or payment authority.
- `index.ts`: Five-language copy loading and centralized value interpolation.
- `catalogs/`: `en.ts`, `zh-CN.ts`, `ja.ts`, `es.ts`, `fr.ts`; identical service and policy keys.

The active snapshot is unavailable for purchase. A live Stripe account is not a configured price, a working BILL-12 backend or a launch authorization. Final policy v1.0 is incorporated in all five catalogs: inclusive web tax, end-of-period cancellation and cycle changes, three-day eligible renewal grace, no restored trial after refunds, and distinct cloud/deletion/backup/record deadlines. Policy approval alone cannot open sales; provider settings and lifecycle must still be verified. Payments and eligibility remain a trusted-backend responsibility.

Lifecycle copy remains launch-scoped: retrieval produces an independent local copy, replacement needs an explicit choice and protective backup, and account sign-in does not replace decryption credentials. App-only status/reminders do not extend deadlines. Online deletion and the supplier's 35-day backup ceiling remain launch requirements, not verified current capabilities. Grace starts at the original renewal deadline. No account or deletion API is added to the static website.
